Neighborhood Playdate · last updated 26 September 2026

The short version

Neighborhood Playdate helps a parent arrange a first playdate with a family nearby, inside a week, without a group chat. You post when you're free, see which families on your block overlap, and send one invitation with a time and a public place. Once you both say yes, the rest happens in Messages.

Questions, problems, or a family to report: julien.lerosty@gmail.com. A person reads every message.

Joining

You need an invite code. Playdate opens one block at a time, and every family joins with a code from a neighbor who is already on. Ask them to open Settings → Invite a neighbor. A code works once, so each new family needs its own.

You sign in with your phone number. We text you a six-digit code; there is no password. If the code doesn't arrive within a minute, check the number and ask for a new one.

If you see "Not on this block yet", your area hasn't opened. We keep a rough, 200-metre version of your location so we can tell you when a block opens near you, and nothing else.

Using it

  • Post a window. The hours you're actually free, and what you'd like to do. Windows vanish once they pass.
  • See who overlaps. Families on your block whose free hours cross yours, closest first.
  • Propose. One time, one public place. They have 24 hours to answer.
  • Say hello. After you both accept, you each get the other's phone number once, and Messages takes over.
  • Afterwards, one question: did it happen? It is the only thing the app keeps score of.

Notifications

Playdate sends three kinds: a new proposal, a yes, and the evening question about whether a playdate happened. If they stop arriving, check Settings → Notifications → Playdate on your iPhone.

Safety

  • First playdates are always at a public spot. Homes unlock only after you've met once.
  • Children are an age range and, if you choose, a gender. Never a name, a birthday, or a photo.
  • Your address never leaves your phone. It is rounded to a 200-metre square before anything is sent.
  • Block or report from the menu on any match or proposal. Blocking is quiet and mutual: neither family sees the other again, and nobody is told. A report reaches us straight away. Manage blocks under Settings → Blocked families.

Deleting your account

Settings → Delete my account removes your family, your children's age ranges, your windows, your playdates and your sign-in, at once and permanently. There is no separate request to make.
