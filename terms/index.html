Who can use it

Neighborhood Playdate is for parents and legal guardians aged 18 or over. Children do not use it and cannot have an account. Use your own phone number and your own first name, and give your children's real age ranges.

What isn't allowed

There is zero tolerance for objectionable content and abusive behavior. That includes:

  • Offensive, sexual, hateful, threatening or discriminatory words, in a name, a suggested spot, a report, or anywhere else.
  • Harassing, pressuring or frightening another family, in the app or at a playdate.
  • Pretending to be someone else, or joining for anyone but your own family.
  • Using the app to sell, to recruit, or for anything other than arranging playdates.
  • Passing another family's phone number or address on to anyone.
  • Trying to work out where a family lives, or to get around a block.

Reports and removal

Use Report a problem in the menu on any family's card, or write to julien.lerosty@gmail.com. Every report is looked at within 24 hours. The operator may remove any content, and suspend or delete any account, that breaks these terms, without notice. A family removed this way may not rejoin.

Blocking is always available and needs no reason.

Meeting safely

  • First playdates happen at a public spot. The app enforces this; keep to the spirit of it too.
  • Parents supervise their own children. A parent stays unless both families have agreed to a drop-off.
  • Trust your judgment. If something feels wrong, don't go, or leave.

Neighborhood Playdate arranges introductions between families. It does not verify anyone's identity, run background checks, or supervise anyone, and it is not present at any playdate.
